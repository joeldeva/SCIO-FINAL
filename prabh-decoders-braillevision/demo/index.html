How It Works

From camera capture to spoken text in milliseconds

📷

Capture

Photograph Braille under oblique side-lighting. Dots cast shadows making them detectable.

🔍

Detect

YOLOv8 identifies each Braille cell with bounding boxes and classifies it as a letter (a–z).

📝

Decode

Reading-order algorithm sorts detections into lines, left-to-right, inserting spaces at gaps.

🔊

Speak

Decoded text is spoken aloud via TTS — making Braille accessible to sighted readers.
